About 9Seven Group

We deliver with care.

Established in 2017 by Thato Sebongile Nhlapo, 9Seven Group (Pty) Ltd is based in Florida, Gauteng. We support public and private clients with practical services, hands-on management and accountable delivery.

Our work also supports black economic empowerment, local employment and skills transfer, creating opportunities for previously disadvantaged communities.

Competent, supervised teams

We match each job with capable people and a supervisor who takes responsibility for the work.

Safety and quality controls

Safe working practices, suitable tools and workmanship checks guide delivery. Qualified personnel handle regulated work, with specialist sign-off arranged where required.

Clear communication and accountable handover

We agree the scope, keep you informed and provide completion notes or service records at handover.

Our approach & experience

Clear steps. Responsible delivery.

Every engagement begins with an agreed scope and ends with a careful handover—so clients can see what is happening and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We confirm your requirements, site conditions, budget and timeframe, then agree the scope.

  2. 02

    Deliver

    Our supervised teams carry out the work with safety controls and regular quality checks.

  3. 03

    Handover

    We inspect the completed work, resolve snags and provide the records needed for sign-off.
